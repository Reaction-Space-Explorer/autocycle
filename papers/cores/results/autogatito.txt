autogatito, the implementation Golnik and co-workers publish with the circuit
method, run on three networks converted to SBML. Its cores are sorted into single
circuits and superpositions by papers/cores/scripts/autogatito_check.py, then
compared against the enumeration here core for core, on the species and the
reactions both, not on counts.

1,901 cores in all, to twelve species. At three, five and six species nothing is on
either side alone. The first superpositions appear at six, and by twelve they are
half of what is found, which dates the caveat that the counts above three species
are counts of cores that are single circuits. formose G3 is not in
circuit_scope.txt and five and six species are not tested there, so this extends
that comparison to a fifth network and two further sizes.

glucose G3 and formose+ammonia G3, bounded (-b 8 -e 16)

  network              n   autogatito   circuits   superpositions   ours
  glucose G3           3            9          9                0      9
  glucose G3           4          172        145               27    176 exhaustive
  formose+ammonia G3   3           38         38                0     38
  formose+ammonia G3   4           38         38                0     38

formose+ammonia G3 agrees exactly at both sizes, which confirms the two rows
circuit_scope.txt already reports for it with the authors' own code.

glucose G3 at four species does not agree, and the disagreement is specific. Its
172 are a strict subset of the 176 the exhaustive search finds: four cores are
found here and not there, and nothing is found there and not here. Those four are
one species set under four reaction assignments, every reaction consuming one core
species, and all four are circuits this search returns, which is why the circuit
counts stand at 145 against 149. The 27 superpositions are exactly the 27 that
circuit_scope.txt records as missed, so the two searches agree on what a circuit
search cannot reach.

The four are cores. Checked from first principles rather than from is_core, the
matrix is square with determinant -3 and the program of Proposition 4 returns
t* = 0.125 at v = (0.375, 0.25, 0.125, 0.25), giving net production 0.125 on every
core species, so every one of them is strictly amplified.

What separates them is the determinant. Every core autogatito returns for glucose
G3 at four species has |det| 1 or 2, 128 and 44 of them, and the four it does not
return are the only ones with |det| 3. Across every comparison that agreed, no core
has |det| above 2: glucose G3 at three species is 9 cores all at 1, formose G3 at
three, five and six species is 24, 16 and 57 all at 1, and formose+ammonia G3 at
four species is 26 at 1 and 12 at 2. The only comparison containing a |det| 3 core
is the only one that disagrees. A reaction returning two copies of a core species
is what raises the determinant, and it is the last row of the matrix above.

The bound is not the explanation. formose+ammonia G3 was run under the same
-b 8 -e 16 at the same core size and returned all 38, so a bound that leaves that
network complete at four species is not what removes four cores from glucose.

This is a pattern across seven comparisons with a visible mechanism, not a proof
about their algorithm, and it is reported as such.

The conversion writes 578 of the 580 reactions for formose G3 and 1,793 of the
1,795 for formose+ammonia G3. The dropped reactions have no non-food reactant or no
non-food product, and a core reaction must both consume and produce a non-food core
species, so none can sit in a core. glucose G3 drops none.

Run as:
  python partitionNetwork.py -i net.xml -t 16 -o name
  AUTOGATITO_DUMP=cores.pkl python partitionAnalysis.py -x net.xml \
      -i PickleFiles/name/partitionTree0.pkl -b 8 -n True -s name \
      -t 16 -e 16 -c -p -o cycleData
-e bounds the size of the cycles assembled, not -b, and leaving it at 1000 sets
that bound to 500, which is why glucose G3 does not finish unbounded. Note that -p
spawns far more workers than -t asks for.
