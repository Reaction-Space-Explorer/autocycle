autogatito, the implementation Golnik and co-workers publish with the circuit
method, run on formose G3 converted to SBML. Its cores are sorted into single
circuits and superpositions by papers/cores/scripts/autogatito_check.py, and the
circuits are then compared against the enumeration here core for core, on the
species and the reactions both, not on counts.

1,901 cores in all, to twelve species. At three, five and six species nothing is
on either side alone. The first superpositions appear at six, and by twelve they
are half of what is found, which dates the caveat that the counts above three
species are counts of cores that are single circuits.

formose G3 is not in circuit_scope.txt and five and six species are not tested
there, so this extends that comparison to a fifth network and to two further
sizes.

The conversion writes 578 of the 580 reactions. The two it drops have no non-food
reactant or no non-food product, and a core reaction must both consume and produce
a non-food core species, so neither can sit in a core. glucose G3 drops none.

Run as:
  python partitionNetwork.py -i net.xml -t 4 -o name
  AUTOGATITO_DUMP=cores.pkl python partitionAnalysis.py -x net.xml \
      -i PickleFiles/name/partitionTree0.pkl -b 1000 -n True -s name \
      -t 4 -e 1000 -c -p -o cycleData
-e bounds the size of the cycles assembled, not -b, and leaving it at 1000 sets
that bound to 500, which is why a network the size of glucose G3 does not finish.
