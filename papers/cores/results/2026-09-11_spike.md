## Anchored enumeration

A cycle whose every reaction consumes one core species and produces one has
column sums of zero over the core, so the all-ones row annihilates it and the
matrix is singular. Every autocatalytic core therefore contains at least one
*amplifying* reaction, one with a net gain in core species. In Formose G6 those
are 63,024 of 306,145 reactions (20.6%), and in Glucose G5 10,699 of 120,952
(8.8%), so anchoring the search on them and meeting in the middle replaces
walking every cycle.

| | brute force | anchored |
|---|---|---|
| Formose G6, 3 species | 258,051 cycles, 68 s | 189,809 candidates, 34 s |
| Glucose G5, 3 species | 117,163 cycles, 16 s | 78,905 candidates, 7.6 s |

Both return identical results: Formose 2,191 cores / 1,001 distinct / 1,148 I +
1,043 II; Glucose 1,042 / 293 / 695 I + 347 II. Validated against the brute
force on Formose G3, where both give 24 cores.

One bug found and fixed on the way: the first version kept a single reaction per
predecessor when closing a 4-cycle, silently dropping parallel edges into the
closing species. Mean transformations per species pair is 1.03, so the undercount
would have been small and invisible.

## Streamed, and validated against MOD's own accounting

Each cycle is emitted only from the smallest amplifying reaction it contains, so
it is generated once and verified in chunks. Peak memory on Formose G6 at four
species is 442 MB, and candidates fell from 9,470,434 to 4,872,344 for the same
answer.

| network | species | cores | distinct | types | time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formose G6 | 3 | 2,191 | 1,001 | 1148 I, 1043 II | 32 s |
| Formose G6 | 4 | 45,869 | 15,395 | 12369 I, 33120 II, 380 III | 315 s |
| Glucose G5 | 3 | 1,042 | 293 | 695 I, 347 II | 8 s |
| Glucose G5 | 4 | 23,961 | 4,656 | 9504 I, 14354 II, 103 III | 102 s |

The MOD dumps shipped with the networks reconcile the reaction counts exactly:

| | MOD numVertices | ours | MOD numEdges | net-zero | distinct |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formose G6 | 117,874 | 117,874 | 266,877 | 99 | 266,778 |
| Glucose G5 | 48,403 | 48,403 | 100,268 | 45 | 100,223 |

numEdges minus the reactions whose net stoichiometry is zero equals our distinct
transformation count, on both networks, exactly. The Neo4j export lists the same
hyperedge once per rule that derives it, which is why raw reaction counts
overstate the network by 15% (formose) and 21% (glucose). Distinct is the unit
MOD itself counts, so it is the unit to report.
